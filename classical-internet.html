How Virtual Purchases Work

The database checks your signed-in identity, pending purchase request, active plan and wallet balance. A successful purchase records a completed virtual-wallet transaction and creates a subscription with an expiry date.

If your balance is insufficient, the purchase will fail without deducting funds. A pending request alone does not activate a subscription.
